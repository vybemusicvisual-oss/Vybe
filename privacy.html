Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vybe processes information when you use its locally operated video creation and publishing features.

1. Information Vybe Processes

Vybe may process project titles, prompts, captions, lyrics, images, audio, videos, local file paths, publishing preferences, connected-account identifiers, publication status, and technical error information. This information is used to create media and perform actions requested by the user.

2. TikTok Account Information

When you connect TikTok, Vybe may receive your TikTok open identifier, creator username, display name, granted permissions, available privacy options, interaction settings, maximum permitted video duration, publication identifier, and publication status.

Vybe does not receive or store your TikTok password. Authentication is handled by TikTok through its official authorization service.

3. OAuth Tokens

TikTok refresh tokens are encrypted and stored on the user’s local device. They are used only to obtain access tokens needed for creator-authorized actions. Access and refresh tokens are not intentionally displayed in the user interface or included in public project files.

4. Video Publishing

Videos, captions, privacy selections, interaction settings, and required content disclosures are sent to TikTok only after the user explicitly reviews and confirms a publishing action. TikTok processes submitted information according to its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Local Data Storage

Vybe is designed to operate locally. Project files, settings, publication records, and encrypted account authorizations are stored on the user’s device. Vybe does not operate a centralized user-profile database for this local installation.

6. Data Sharing

Vybe sends information to a connected platform only as necessary to complete an action requested by the user. Information may also be processed by user-selected media or AI providers when their features are used. Vybe does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for targeted advertising.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains on the local device until it is deleted by the user. Disconnecting TikTok removes the locally stored TikTok authorization used by Vybe. Local project and publication records may remain until the user deletes them or removes the application’s local data. Users may also revoke Vybe’s access through their TikTok account settings.

8. Security

Vybe uses reasonable safeguards appropriate for a local application, including encryption of stored refresh tokens. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their computer, operating-system account, local files, and connected accounts.

9. Third-Party Services

Vybe may connect to services such as TikTok, YouTube, media providers, and AI providers. Those services process information under their own privacy policies. Vybe does not control their security, retention practices, service availability, or independent use of information.

10. Children’s Privacy

Vybe is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Users must satisfy the age requirements of each connected third-party platform.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Vybe’s functionality, legal obligations, or third-party integrations change. The effective date will be revised when an updated policy is published.

12. Contact

Questions or requests concerning this policy may be directed to the operator through the GitHub profile that publishes this website.
